# Environment: what the engine already does

_Research source, 22 October 2026, for `notes/research/environment.md`. What the engine on `main` (`f7f2b07`) does with weather, storms, seasons, places and shared conditions: the code, then a count on 20 throwaway worlds. Call this run [A]._

## What I ran

`./run.sh notes/research/sources/environment-scripts/dump.ts 9001 9020 10 <dir>` ran 20 throwaway worlds (seeds 9001–9020, the range already used for diagnostics in `docs/seeds.md`) with the default model, the usual past and **10 present years**, so each world is one world-decade. The mean town had 83 people and 67 adults. The script reads the world and changes nothing. `python3 -I notes/research/sources/environment-scripts/analyse.py <dir>` counts each kind; its output is `environment-scripts/analyse-out.txt`. **Leads** and **turn** use the consequence map's line, as `intrigue-scripts/analyse.py` does: a weighty descendant within 36 months through cited causes, and a descendant of a turn kind, followed for sources in the first 7 years. **Witness** is whether the run's witness knows the event at the end; **town knows** is the share of the town that does. Rates are per world-decade with a 95% interval over worlds.

- **The storm is upkeep.** 17 decisions a world-decade that lead somewhere 1% of the time, below the hat (2%) and promises (7–12%) in `intrigue-situations.md`. The accident, the same shape of decision (danger, public, courage or caution), leads 40–50% of the time. The difference is not the event's size: it is that the accident's choice is about a named person who remembers it.
- **Two false lines** (exit point 3). "Hung back when the others went out into the storm" is said in the 5 of 62 storms (8%) where nobody went. "Proud of the day in the storm" is the portrait's name for every rescue: 151 portrait lines in 1,331 living adults, none from a storm.
- **Weather in portraits is common and inert.** 31% of adults carry a weather line (184 loves, 151 "the day in the storm", 119 quirks or counts). No decision reads any of them.
- **No event hits a set of people at once** except death's reach: of 120 events a world-decade naming three or more people, 96 are group rehearsals and shows.
- **Who a shared hardship would find exposed**, at the end, as shares of adults: 75 and over 20% (13 a world), ill this month 3% (2), living alone 36% (24), with no friend 12% (8), alone and friendless 8% (5), alone and 75 or over 9% (6).

## Limits

One run of 20 worlds on one setting; the town is a placeholder and these are its rates, not targets. Event counts include only logged decisions: routine answers repeated unchanged are sometimes not logged (`grammar.ts:500–508`), which here changes little (5.5 logged deciders per storm against 5.6 harbour staff). "Exposed" is a snapshot at the end of the run.
