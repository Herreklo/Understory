# Intrigue: which situations make stories, and which the engine makes

_Research source, 22 October 2026. The question: what makes a simulated life intriguing, and which kinds of situation make stories? Part (a) distils fifteen situation kinds from story theory and games [1–12], sorted into engine (general to any setting with people) and domain. Part (b) audits the engine against them, from reports on file and one diagnostic run. Part (c) is a shortlist of four, each with the smallest mechanism in people that would produce it._

**The hard rule, kept in every line below.** Storiness must not become scripting: no decision reads a story measure, and patterns are found only afterwards (`notes/game-shapes.md`, rule 4). Every recommendation here names the state in people and the situation that reads it. None names a target rate.

## What I ran

`./run.sh notes/research/sources/intrigue-scripts/dump.ts 9001 9020 8 <dir>` ran 20 throwaway worlds (seeds 9001–9020, the range `docs/seeds.md` already gives density diagnostics), with the default model, the usual 30-year past and 8 present years. The mean town was 83 people. The script dumps the present record. For every event with two people it adds whether, at that moment, they were friends, partners or kin, and how many people were friends of both. `python3 -I notes/research/sources/intrigue-scripts/analyse.py <dir>` counts each kind. It uses the consequence map's definitions (`src/tools/consequence-defs.ts`): **leads** means a weighty descendant within 36 months, through cited causes, and **turn** means a descendant of a turn kind; both are followed only for sources in the first five years. **Known** means the witness knows the event at the end of the run. Rates are per world-decade with a 95% interval over worlds. Call this run [R5]. Other numbers come from `reports/density-22oct.md` [R1], `reports/consequences-f26.txt` [R2], `docs/STATUS.md` [R3] and `notes/research/feelings-and-traits.md` [R4].

**Domain kinds**, left out of the engine list: the crime pursued and its investigation (P3, the detective), revolt and war (P6, P8), abduction (P10), the monster (B), conflict with a god (P31), madness (P16, a clinical domain), the daring enterprise and the escape (P9; T adventure, escape), and winning a match, a show or an election. Each needs the domain's stakes and rules. The engine supplies what people do about them: rivalry, betrayal, debts, sides.

**What the sources agree on (inferred).** Across Polti, Tobias, Booker and Propp, the situations that recur are relations and not events: two people and a third thing (a good, a person, a secret, a debt) whose claims cannot all be met. Labov adds that a story is told when the event is rare and morally charged [5]. The games add that the story is told when the player can see a named cause, a named object and a delay: CK3's hook is a secret held and used later [9], RimWorld's social fight cites an insult [10], and Kreminski's patterns are always sequences with an `unless` [6, 7]. What the engine lacks is that **third term, held across time by a person**.

## What the audit says

- **Most present kinds are dyadic and immediate.** A wrong is answered within the month (30.6 retaliations a decade), but nobody keeps it for an opening, so the grudge's acts are 9% known. A secret is kept by 12.7% of adults in any month, and only the holder ever decides about it.
- **Volume is not story.** Promises (100 a world-decade) and ill parents (31) are the commonest "situations" here and lead nowhere, 7–12% of the time, with the witness knowing 3–6%. Owning up happens 27 times a decade and leads nowhere, ever. These are upkeep, like the hat (2% [R2]).
- **The strongest kinds are positional or about ties ending:** succession (78–80% turn, 98% known), death (62% turn, 99% known), making up (79% turn). They reach because a role or a tie that others depend on changes [R1].
- **The missing kinds are general, not domain:** sides, a third person in love, inheritance, and debts that come from acts. The state behind three of them already exists (friends of both, secrets with `knownBy`, savings at death, help given).

## (c) The shortlist: general, missing or weak, and most likely to raise intrigue

Each item adds a state that persists in a person and a situation that reads it. Nothing reads a story measure, and the consequences follow from bonds and beliefs alone.

1. **Taking sides** (kind 6; it also gives 2 and 12 reach). **State:** none new. It reuses the rift event and the friends of both, which [R5] counts at 39 rifts in five years. **Mechanism:** after a rivalry is declared, a split or a betrayal, a friend of both meets a situation to side with A, side with B or keep out. It is weighed by liking and regard for each, by the accounts they have heard (`telling.ts`, bent by the teller, so who told them first matters) and by their fairness. The side not taken loses liking and trust, and the side taken remembers. Friendships then end for a reason that reaches past the pair, where friendship's reach is 0% today [R1]. This is the cheapest of the four, because the state is already there.
2. **What is owed, called in** (kinds 4 and 7; demand map L). **State:** `owes` (declared, never read) gets its job. It is set by costly help that really happened (`give` at a request, a rescue, money from family, coming to an ill parent) and by an unrepaired wrong, as resentment [R4]. **Mechanism:** when the creditor next needs something the debtor can give, through a request, backing for a plan, a contest's decider or a helper, they turn to the debtor first. The debtor weighs the debt, citing its event. The wronged weigh their wrong in the same situations, so revenge waits for its opening instead of snapping the same month. This replaces the random promise, which [R5] shows is noise.
3. **Knowing someone's secret** (kinds 3 and 2). **State:** `knownBy` on a secret act already records who knows it. **Mechanism:** a knower who meets the secret's owner in a hostile or contested situation (a grudge, a rivalry, a contest, being criticised) is offered "bring it out", weighed by honesty-humility and the bond. Exposure runs the path `caught` already uses: it is public, and bonds move. An owner who believes the other knows weighs exposure in their own choices toward them, by appeasing or avoiding. This is CK3's hook [9] without a director.
4. **A third person** (kinds 14, 2 and 3). **State:** attraction to someone other than one's partner. It is built from the liking and chemistry the engine already has, it persists, and it grows where a couple's distance and doubts are high (`love.ts`). **Mechanism:** a decision to act on it or not. An affair is a secret act, so it feeds item 3. When it comes out, it is a betrayal that feeds the partner's doubts and leaving, and the friends' sides (item 1). Rule 8 holds: the obstacle is a tie and an act, never an identity.

**Runners-up.** Inheritance (kind 10): savings already pass silently. A house or a business that cannot be split would turn siblings into claimants, but it needs property beyond savings. The linked comeback (kind 8): occasions already open a want after a blow, yet 3% of rises descend from one. Part of that may be the record losing the chain at the want ([R2]: "a want records no event it came from"), so check it before building anything.

## This fails if …

1. **Sides become upkeep, as the hat did.** Measure: the share of side-taking decisions that end or start a friendship, or are cited by a weighty event, within 3 years. Stop below 15% (the hat is at 2% [R2]).
2. **Debts decide nothing, as round 32's ledger decided under 1%** [R3]. Measure: the share of decisions where the debt is weighed and removing it flips the family. Stop below 5% (the same line as [R4]'s fails-if 2).
3. **The new acts stay invisible.** Measure: the share of leverage, exposure and side-taking acts the witness knows. Today secret acts are at 8% and promises at 3%, so stop below 30%.
4. **Everyone is exposed or cheating.** Measure: the share of adults exposed, or in an affair, per decade, switch on against off. Stop if either passes 10%, or if splits rise outside the off arm's interval without the round saying so.
5. **The new kinds crowd out the old ones.** One situation a month means a new kind displaces another. Measure: kinds of line per life ([R3], weakness 3) must rise, and the dead-life share (2%) must not.
