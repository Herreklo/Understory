# Feelings and traits: research

In progress. Research for kinds of feeling (each from how an event is seen, each pulling toward different acts, handled according to personality), and for which properties and skills a person needs. Prompted by the human pilot reading of 22 October 2026, which found lives samey and reasons that never say what was felt.
