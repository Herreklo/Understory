Person Model Lab

A small town, simulated for thirty years before you arrive and ten more after. Nothing is scripted: situations are matched against the world and answered through each person's traits, if-then signatures and self-story. Every event says why it happened. Read each person from the felt side, which shows only what one witness in town could know, or the truth side, which shows everything. The gap between the two is the game.
