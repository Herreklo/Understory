Øystein played `src/tools/play.ts` on world 4 (main at `ce27dca`, round 45's feelings in) as Mona Berry, 59, a singer who works at the clinic. He stopped after a few minutes: "this is unplayable. the format. the boredom."

## What the screen showed

- **The opening is an inventory, not a situation.** `p` lists 21 lines, twelve of them "You are fond of …", most of them people past 70, with open nights from 2019 to 2025. Nothing asks the player anything.
- **The player's person acts without the player.** The first month opens with "Mona made a joke at Victor's expense in front of everyone at the clinic, and nobody laughed." The engine made the witness's decision, and the player read about it.
- **There is one thing to do:** invite someone to a rehearsal. Every other command reads, and a month is a key press.
- **Rough edges:** `i` without an id answers "nobody has the id NaN"; two runs ended at the first prompt.

## What it says

The boredom is not the format. With no decision of their own, a player has no stake, and the client is a cold page with a pager: the "police log" of 22 October again. Polishing the text would not change that. Nothing here says the people are wrong; it says nobody can care about them from a seat with nothing to decide.
