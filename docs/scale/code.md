# How the engine scales: what lives where

A city is the engine with neighbourhoods. In a town of one neighbourhood every scale path is the engine's own code, with the same arithmetic in the same order and the same dice: each accessor returns the engine's scan, and each city path sits behind `hoods(w) > 1`. A city exists only when a harness or a game asks for one (`SCALE_FOCUS`, `SCALE_CITY`). The identity checks (`src/tools/diag/ident.ts` and `scale/identity2.ts`) hash a whole town run, every field of every person, every event, bond and belief, and the groups, workplaces and random streams, to show it.

## New files

- **scale.ts: settings.**
  - Neighbourhoods: about 80 people each, fixed when the town is built. Households live together, and a household moves when people move in with each other.
  - A membership index for a city: who is in town, staff by workplace, residents by neighbourhood, and who is jobless. It is kept beside the world, not in it, so it is never hashed or copied. A change of job updates it in place (`jobSet`); a change of presence marks it for rebuilding (`moved()`).
  - Lookups: `town` (everyone in town, the engine's own scan), `staffOf` (a workplace's staff), `near` (the neighbourhoods of some people), `around` (someone's neighbourhood, workplace, circle, friends and family), `aroundAll`, `jobless`. In a town each returns the engine's own scan.
  - Institutions: `makeInstances` gives a neighbourhood one workplace of each kind; `typeOf` is a workplace's kind; `placeOf` finds the instance of a kind in a neighbourhood; `hoodName` names a neighbourhood in plain English parts.
  - The background list: background people are kept apart from `w.people`, so nothing that runs each month walks past them. Their bonds are put away with them (`stowBonds`) and come back on promotion (`fromBackground`).
- **coarse.ts: the background of a city.**
  - The yearly pass. Person-local rules run by the engine's own formulas month by month: growing older (`mature`, shared with the engine's yearly step), health, illness, death, retirement, the person's own strain, and becoming a parent. Rules that depend on other people use odds from coarse-odds.ts: partnering, splitting, a child, leaving or coming home, drinking, losing a job, savings.
  - Background workplaces (`bgPlaces`): yearly hiring from the neighbourhood's job seekers, succession with the town's events (promotion, first staff meeting, passed over), and newcomers for slots nobody local can fill, by the town's newcomer rule.
  - Independence: ids from a range per neighbourhood, first names unique among the neighbourhood's residents, partners from the neighbourhood, every draw keyed to a person or a neighbourhood and year.
  - Background events carry the town's weight for their kind and how widely the town would have seen them (`seen`); a married couple that separates divorces.
  - A city's background at its founding (`populateBackground`): households shaped like the town's founding households, with each neighbourhood's workplaces filled up to capacity.
  - Promotion of a neighbourhood (`promoteHood`), with its workplaces.
- **coarse-odds.ts** is generated by `scale/bench/fit.py` from full-engine person-years. It is never edited by hand, and is refitted whenever the engine changes.

## The engine's files in a city

- **Building (build.ts):** a city builds one set of workplaces per neighbourhood, and careers at year 0 are found in one's own neighbourhood. Circles are drawn from one's own neighbourhood. In a city split into focus and background (`SCALE_FOCUS`), background workplaces move to `bgPlaces` with their staff and leaders; `SCALE_CITY` puts a town inside a background of a given size. The newcomer rule scales with the size of the monthly world. `SCALE_PAST_SALT` gives the monthly world a different past from the same founding, for the independence test.
- **Bond keys (world.ts):** `a × base + b`. A town keeps base 100,000. A city uses 2²⁶, set when its neighbourhoods are set; the founding families' bonds are re-keyed in their order. `bkA` and `bkB` decode a key for its world.
- **Work (life.ts):** workplaces read their staff lists. A vacancy takes on people from its own neighbourhood first, and hears from at most 24 job seekers, drawn at random; job seekers are the jobless and those after a post elsewhere. A venture opens in its founder's neighbourhood (projects.ts).
- **The monthly social tick (life.ts):** school, hobbies, routines and company sought happen within a person's neighbourhood. Brothers and sisters are found through their parents. Those who serve the public meet the adults of their workplace's neighbourhood.
- **Romance and leaving (life.ts):** someone to fall for, and someone who said they would go too, is someone around the person.
- **News (world.ts):** witnesses at work come from staff lists. Public and local news reach the neighbourhoods of the people involved.
- **Situations, plans, groups, persona, drama, pursuits, steering:** every scan for a person's coworkers uses the staff list; every scan for people who might react to someone uses the people around them. A workplace named by kind (accidents, the fleet, the collection's bar, the boat, the bar's owner, a plan's threat, the music teacher's school) is that kind in the person's neighbourhood. Kin are found through parents.
- **Standing (regard.ts):** averaged over those around the person.
- **Marks (persona.ts):** a mark reads an event's `seen` where it exists, else its `vis`. Only background events have `seen`.
- **Status:** people can be `bg` (in the city, coarse pass) or `gone` (moved away from the city; the coarse pass may bring them home). The monthly systems act only on people `in` town.
- **Catch-up for promotion (life.ts, `catchUp`):** the settings of the promoted are run on a clock set back to when those months happened, so rules that count time see months. These cover home, family, work, school, circle, routines, hobbies, public service and company sought. Who counts as close is refreshed each month, only for people whose bonds can have moved. Anything written to the record in those months is dated to the promotion and marked as backstory.
- **For tests (grammar.ts):** `wiringC`, `identityC` and `pickExpression` are exported, so the twin test can pose the engine's own situations.
