# Reading a life: a short guide for reviewers

Understory simulates a small town: thirty years before the witness arrives, ten after. Nothing is scripted.
Each person is read from two sides. FELT is what one witness in town could know. TRUTH is everything.
The gap between them is the point: a reader of the page only ever gets the felt side.

What to look for, and what to tell us:
1. Could you recognise this person from five of their events, with the name hidden? Which five?
2. Pick a decision. Read its causes. Did they convince you, or did they feel like labels?
3. Did anything repeat in a way a novelist would have cut? Quote it.
4. Did the person contradict themselves in a way that felt human (a situation that brought out another side), or in a way that felt like a bug?
5. Was there a twist you didn't see coming but could have? Was there one that felt like a dice roll?
6. Did the felt side give you enough to form a view of them? Too little? Too much?
7. Anything that made you feel something: laugh, pity, dislike. Name the person and the moment.

Downloads from the page: a whole world (chronicle + every life), one life, or the raw JSON. Source files are listed under "Source files".
Honesty beats kindness here. Quote the text that failed.
